## 🎯 3 Real-Life Case Study Practice Problems

1. **Case Study 1: NovaGen Pharmaceutical Clinical Trial Risk Classification**:
   - Imbalanced clinical trial data, Gradient Boosting vs AdaBoost, learning rate shrinkage tuning, aur ROC-AUC evaluation.
2. **Case Study 2: Microchip Quality Control Defect Detection via AdaBoost**:
   - Step-by-step mathematical tracing of decision stump weights aur sample re-weighting.
3. **Case Study 3: Automated High-Frequency Fraud Detection Pipeline (Stacking vs Blending)**:
   - Out-Of-Fold (OOF) cross-validation vs Holdout Blending architecture sub-15ms production latency ke liye.
