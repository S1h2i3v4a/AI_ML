## 📋 Case Studies Ka Summary

### 🏢 Case 1: SaaS YoY Revenue Growth & Executive Reporting
- **Domain**: B2B SaaS Growth & Business Intelligence
- **Tested Modules**: Lecture 18.01 – 18.07
- **Key Concepts**: Multi-series line plots, fast format strings (`fmt`), DPI calibration, canvas styling, aur Edward Tufte ka Data-Ink ratio.

### 🏢 Case 2: Enterprise Financial Audit & Spending Variance
- **Domain**: Corporate Finance & Operational Analytics
- **Tested Modules**: Lecture 18.08 – 18.12
- **Key Concepts**: Symmetrical grouped bar coordinate mathematics ($X_i \pm \frac{w}{2}$), direct labels via `ax.bar_label()`, aur horizontal bar charts (`plt.barh`).

### 🏢 Case 3: Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) vs Acquisition Cost (CAC) Analysis
- **Domain**: E-Commerce & Unit Economics
- **Tested Modules**: Lecture 18.13 – 18.18
- **Key Concepts**: 4D scatter plot (visual encoding $X, Y$, size $s$, color $c$), direct annotations (`plt.annotate`), aur composition donut charts.
