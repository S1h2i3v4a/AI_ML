## 🎯 3 Real-Life Case Study Practice Problems

1. **Case Study 1: E-Commerce Customer Purchasing Behavior Segmentation**:
   - $N=25,000$ loyalty customers ka spending data, WCSS Inertia decay, Silhouette score $s(i)$ ka numerical calculation, aur `KneeLocator` se automated elbow finding.
2. **Case Study 2: Cancer Genomic Microarray Clustering**:
   - $N=120$ tumor biopsy samples, Single vs Complete vs Ward linkage, Chaining effect prevention, aur Dendrogram distance cut threshold ($	heta$) se cancer subtypes nikalna.
3. **Case Study 3: Urban Ride-Sharing GPS Anomaly & Hotspot Detection**:
   - Non-linear curved waterfront roads par K-Means ka failure, DBSCAN Epsilon ($\epsilon = 250\text{m}$) aur MinPts ($8$) se Core, Border aur Noise (-1) alag karna.
