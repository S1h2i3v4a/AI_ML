## 📌 Module Overview

**Lecture 20** Artificial Intelligence aur Machine Learning ke liye **Probability Theory** ka rigorous mathematical foundation establish karta hai.
Real-world data hamesha noisy aur uncertain hota hai. Classical deterministic rule-based systems yahan fail ho jate hain.
Sample spaces aur Kolmogorov's axioms se lekar Bayes' Theorem aur parametric distributions (Binomial, Uniform, Gaussian) tak, yeh module AI models ko train aur calibrate karne ke saare zaroori probability concepts ko deeply cover karta hai.

### Mukhya Pillars:
- **Foundations & Set Theory:** Random experiments, sample space, events, aur Kolmogorov ke 3 axioms.
- **Probability Calculus:** Complement rule, addition rule, multiplication rule, aur chain rule.
- **Conditional Inference:** Conditional probability, Law of Total Probability, aur Bayesian belief updating.
- **Random Variables & Moments:** Discrete/Continuous variables, PMF, PDF, CDF, Mean $\mathbb{E}[X]$, aur Variance $\text{Var}(X)$.
- **Parametric Distributions:** Binomial, Continuous Uniform, aur Normal (Gaussian) Distribution with Z-scores.

## 📐 Ganitiya Sutra (Mathematical Foundations Reference)

### 1. Kolmogorov ke Teeno Axioms
Sample space $\mathcal{S}$ aur event space $\mathcal{F}$ ke liye:

$$
\boxed{P(A) \ge 0 \quad \forall A \subseteq \mathcal{S}}
$$

$$
\boxed{P(\mathcal{S}) = 1.0}
$$

$$
\boxed{A_i \cap A_j = \emptyset \quad \forall i \ne j \implies P\left(\bigcup_{i=1}^\infty A_i\right) = \sum_{i=1}^\infty P(A_i)}
$$

---

### 2. General Addition aur Multiplication Rules

$$
\boxed{P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cap B)}
$$

$$
\boxed{P(A \cap B) = P(A) \cdot P(B \mid A) = P(B) \cdot P(A \mid B)}
$$

---

### 3. Bayes' Theorem aur Law of Total Probability
Sample space partition $\{B_1, \dots, B_k\}$ aur observed evidence $E$ ke liye:

$$
\boxed{P(E) = \sum_{j=1}^k P(E \mid B_j) \cdot P(B_j)}
$$

$$
\boxed{P(B_i \mid E) = \frac{P(E \mid B_i) \cdot P(B_i)}{\sum_{j=1}^k P(E \mid B_j) \cdot P(B_j)}}
$$

---

### 4. Continuous Gaussian (Normal) Distribution
Random variable $X \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma^2)$ ke liye:

$$
\boxed{f(x) = \frac{1}{\sigma \sqrt{2\pi}} \exp\left( -\frac{(x - \mu)^2}{2\sigma^2} \right) \qquad\text{aur}\qquad Z = \frac{X - \mu}{\sigma}}
$$
