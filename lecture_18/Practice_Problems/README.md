## 📋 Case Studies Summary

### 🏢 Case 1: SaaS YoY Revenue Growth & Executive Reporting
- **Domain**: B2B SaaS Growth & Business Intelligence
- **Tested Modules**: Lecture 18.01 – 18.07
- **Key Focus**: Multi-series line plots, format strings (`fmt`), DPI calibration, canvas styling, and Edward Tufte's Data-Ink ratio.

### 🏢 Case 2: Enterprise Financial Audit & Spending Variance
- **Domain**: Corporate Finance & Operational Analytics
- **Tested Modules**: Lecture 18.08 – 18.12
- **Key Focus**: Symmetrical grouped bar chart coordinate math ($X_i \pm \frac{w}{2}$), direct labeling with `ax.bar_label()`, and horizontal bar layout (`plt.barh`).

### 🏢 Case 3: Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) vs Acquisition Cost (CAC) Analysis
- **Domain**: E-Commerce & Unit Economics
- **Tested Modules**: Lecture 18.13 – 18.18
- **Key Focus**: 4D scatter plots (encoding metrics via $X, Y$, marker size $s$, and colormap $c$), pinpoint annotations (`plt.annotate`), and composition donut charts.
