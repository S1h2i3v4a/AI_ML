## 📌 Curriculum Overview
Welcome to **Lecture 30: Boosting & Advanced Ensembles (Supervised ML Part-6)**! This module covers the mathematical foundations, classroom blackboard proofs, and industry implementations of sequential boosting and multi-model heterogeneous ensembles.

Topics include **Gradient Tree Boosting (Regression & Classification)**, **AdaBoost**, **XGBoost**, **LightGBM**, **CatBoost**, **Hard & Soft Voting**, **Out-of-Fold (OOF) Stacking**, **Blending**, and the **NovaGen Research Labs Clinical Trial Project**.

## 🎯 3 Strict Case-Based Practice Problems (Syllabus Aligned)

1. **Case Study 1: NovaGen Pharmaceutical Clinical Trial Risk Classification**:
   - Imbalanced clinical trial data, Gradient Boosting vs AdaBoost, learning rate shrinkage tuning, and ROC-AUC evaluation.
2. **Case Study 2: Microchip Quality Control Defect Detection via AdaBoost**:
   - Step-by-step mathematical tracing of decision stump weights and sample re-weighting with normalisation factor Z_m.
3. **Case Study 3: High-Frequency Real-Time Fraud Detection Pipeline (Stacking vs Blending)**:
   - Out-Of-Fold (OOF) cross-validation vs Holdout Blending architecture to satisfy sub-15ms inference latency constraints.
